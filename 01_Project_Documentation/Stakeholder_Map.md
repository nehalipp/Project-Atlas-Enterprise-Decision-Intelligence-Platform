# Project Atlas: Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform

# Stakeholder Map

## Document Purpose

This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in Project Atlas, their responsibilities, data ownership areas, analytical needs, and expected interaction with the enterprise intelligence platform.

Understanding stakeholder requirements ensures that the platform delivers meaningful business value and supports effective decision-making.

---

# Stakeholder Overview

Project Atlas involves stakeholders across executive leadership, business departments, technology teams, and operational functions.

| Stakeholder Group | Role | Primary Interest |
|---|---|---|
| Executive Leadership | Strategic decision makers | Enterprise performance visibility |
| Finance Team | Financial analysis and planning | Profitability and cost management |
| Sales Team | Revenue generation | Customer and sales performance |
| Supply Chain Team | Logistics and procurement | Inventory and supplier optimization |
| Operations Team | Operational execution | Efficiency and quality improvement |
| HR Team | Workforce management | Employee insights |
| Sustainability Team | ESG reporting | Environmental performance |
| Data Team | Platform development | Data reliability and governance |
| Business Analysts | Analytics delivery | Reporting and insights |

---

- Which business areas are performing well?
- Where are risks emerging?
- What opportunities require investment?

## Analytics Requirements

- Revenue Growth
- Profitability
- Market Performance
- Operational Efficiency

Executive dashboards and KPI scorecards.

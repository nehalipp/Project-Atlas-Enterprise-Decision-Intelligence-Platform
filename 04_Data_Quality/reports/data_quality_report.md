## Customer Dataset Assessment

Status:
⚠ Requires Data Cleaning

Findings:

1. Dataset contains 51,000 records.
2. 50,000 unique customer IDs identified.
3. 986 exact duplicate records detected.
4. Approximately 5% missing values found in:
   - Industry
   - Customer Segment

Impact:

Customer segmentation and geographic reporting may produce inaccurate results without remediation.
